### About

I'm a **Data Scientist at Western Digital**, with a background in production data engineering and AI workflows. My interests are moving increasingly toward **deep learning research and engineering**: understanding models from first principles, implementing architectures myself, and developing the skills to train, evaluate, and improve language models.

I've worked on data pipelines, PostgreSQL-backed services, automation, and agentic AI systems. Outside work, I build small, focused implementations to understand **autograd, sequence models, attention, and Transformers** rather than relying only on high-level libraries.

My longer-term focus is **reinforcement learning for language models (RLHF and related post-training methods)**, model evaluation, and efficient ML systems.
